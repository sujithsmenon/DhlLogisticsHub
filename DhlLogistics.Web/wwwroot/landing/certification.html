AUTHORIZATION

Licensed Customs Broker

As a legally accredited Customs Broker by the Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs (CBIC), PVGT holds the regulatory authority to clear freight seamlessly across Indian seaports and airports.

  • Strict adherence to EXIM policies and international trade compliance.
  • Expedited clearance minimizing border delays and detention costs.
  • Direct EDI access for rapid, paperless documentation filing.

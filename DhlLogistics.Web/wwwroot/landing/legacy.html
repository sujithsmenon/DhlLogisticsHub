1959: The Genesis

  • Founded in 1959 by P.V. George Tharakan.
  • Rooted in the establishment of P.V. George Tharakan and Company.
  • Launched the first Standard Mobil-ESSO retail outlet (1959–1967).

1961: Breaking New Ground

  • Established as the first Jawa/Yezdi motorcycle dealer in Kerala.
  • Held exclusive rights in the Ernakulam District from 1961 to 1998.
  • Founded Fitwell Caps Pvt. Ltd. in 1971, directing manufacturing operations until 1986.

1966: The Logistics Pivot

  • Formally entered the maritime trade sector in 1966.
  • Commenced operations as a licensed Customs House Agent (CHA) in Kochi.
  • Established the operational foundation for our current shipping and port capabilities.

1986: Anchoring the Logistics

  • Executed a strategic shift in 1986 to focus exclusively on logistics as our core business.
  • Leveraged our established network to build a reliable distribution infrastructure.
  • Scaled operations to manage everything from basic parcels to large-scale enterprise freight.

A National Footprint

  • Headquartered at Tharakan House on M.G. Road in Kochi.
  • Expanded our physical presence with operational hubs in Trivandrum, Chennai, Mumbai, Delhi, and Gujarat.
  • Structured to facilitate comprehensive logistics from domestic origins to global destinations.

Global Vision

  • Vision: To serve as a trusted logistics partner connecting businesses worldwide.
  • The corporate identity communicates trust, professionalism, and global connectivity.
  • Driven by a legacy built over six decades of consistent service and reliability.
